Nuestra casa

Three Marias,
one kitchen.

We’re a small family spot on Telephone Road, a few minutes from Hobby — the kind of place you find once and then can’t stop coming back to. Everything starts with the tortilla: corn pressed and cooked on the comal, flour rolled by hand, both made the same day you eat them.

From there it’s Matamoros cooking — trompo al pastor carved to order, quesabirria with consomé for dipping, gorditas, sopes, huaraches, tortas big enough to share, and menudo when the weekend calls for it. Breakfast starts at eight, so yes, you can have tacos on the way to the airport.

Preguntas

Good to know.

Are the tortillas really handmade?

Yes — corn tortillas pressed and cooked on the comal, flour tortillas rolled by hand. It’s the part of the meal we’re proudest of.

Do you take call-in orders?

We do. Call (713) 987-9391, tell us what you want, and we’ll have it waiting at the counter.

Is there delivery?

Yes, through DoorDash. For the best version of everything, come sit down with us.

When do you serve menudo?

Menudo is a weekend tradition here. Call ahead on the day you’re coming and we’ll tell you exactly what’s in the pot.
